Neptune

About Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and farthest known planet from the Sun in the Solar System. It is an ice giant, like Uranus, and has a dynamic atmosphere with the fastest winds in the solar system—reaching speeds of over 2,000 km/h.

Its deep blue color is due to the absorption of red light by methane in its atmosphere. Neptune has a very active climate with huge storms and dark spots similar to Jupiter’s Great Red Spot.

The planet has 14 known moons, the largest of which is Triton—a moon that orbits in the opposite direction of Neptune’s rotation, suggesting it was captured from the Kuiper Belt.

Neptune was the first planet discovered through mathematical prediction rather than direct observation. It was officially identified in 1846 and visited only once by Voyager 2 in 1989.
