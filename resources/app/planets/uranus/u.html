Uranus

About Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and the third largest by diameter. It’s often referred to as an "ice giant" due to its cold, dense atmosphere filled with water, methane, and ammonia. Its bluish-green color comes from methane gas in the upper atmosphere absorbing red light.

Uniquely, Uranus rotates on its side with an axial tilt of 98 degrees. This causes extreme seasons that last over 20 Earth years. It completes a rotation every 17.2 hours and takes 84 Earth years to orbit the Sun once.

Uranus has 13 known rings and at least 27 moons, with names derived from Shakespearean and Alexander Pope characters. The most notable moons include Titania, Oberon, and Miranda.

So far, only one spacecraft—Voyager 2—has visited Uranus in 1986. It revealed a bland atmosphere with minimal cloud activity, but more recent observations suggest dynamic weather and large storms.
