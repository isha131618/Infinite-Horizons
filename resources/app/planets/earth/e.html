About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. Its surface is covered 71% by water and has a breathable atmosphere that sustains millions of species of organisms, including humans.

Earth has a molten core, a dynamic atmosphere, tectonic plates, and a protective magnetic field. Its seasons are caused by its axial tilt, and it orbits the Sun every 365.25 days.

Our planet has one natural satellite, the Moon, which influences tides and stabilizes Earth’s rotation. Earth is the densest planet in the solar system and the fifth-largest in terms of diameter.

Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago and has undergone massive geological and biological transformations. It is the only planet known to have plate tectonics, and its biosphere makes it incredibly unique.
