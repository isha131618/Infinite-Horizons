Mars

About Mars

Mars, known as the Red Planet due to its iron oxide-rich soil, is the fourth planet from the Sun and a major focus of planetary exploration. Slightly over half the diameter of Earth, it has a thin atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide and dramatic features including enormous volcanoes and deep canyons.

The planet experiences seasons similar to Earth due to its tilted axis. It has polar ice caps, dust storms, and signs of ancient water flows that suggest it may have once supported microbial life.

Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos. Robotic missions like Viking, Curiosity, Perseverance, and orbiters like MAVEN and Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter have provided vast amounts of data about the Martian surface and atmosphere.

Mars is a top candidate for future human colonization, with space agencies planning crewed missions in the coming decades.
